# Style Guide

Read this before final prompts or image generation.

## Visual Signature

- Black pen-and-ink linework on pure white.
- Loose, relaxed, handmade strokes rather than vector-perfect contours.
- Light ink wash or sparse hatching only where it improves form.
- Large negative space and one dominant visual action.
- Vermillion red `#E34234` as the recurring identity color.
- Editorial restraint, dry humor, and quiet intelligence.

## Red Contract

- Default to one red area: the recurring analyst's sweater.
- Red must not carry an abstract meaning the scene cannot express in black and white.
- A second red object is allowed only when it is concrete, recognizable, causally important, and approved.
- Reserve red data points for genuine outlier or anomaly topics.
- Never let one red dot mean different things across a series.

## Orange Line Elements

- One image, one idea.
- One visible tension or mismatch.
- Action beats explanation.
- Scale contrast when it sharpens the joke.
- The image should be understood in one glance and reward a second look.

## Detail Budget

- One complex object is allowed; everything around it should become simpler.
- Prefer five to seven strong contour masses over technical rendering.
- Use sparse hatching and broken contour lines.
- Avoid CAD-like precision, engineering plates, dense cross-hatching, and decorative room detail.
- If machinery is the joke, preserve its scale and recognizability but simplify internal parts.

## Series Continuity

- Reuse the same short-haired analyst, simple face, red sweater, and black trousers.
- Keep aspect ratio, red placement, line density, and negative-space target stable.
- Vary secondary characters and metaphor families.
- Use one accepted anchor per batch.
- Generate 2-3 images, inspect drift, then continue.

## No-Title Clarity

Before accepting an image:

- describe the literal action without naming the principle
- state the visible absurdity
- confirm the image works in grayscale
- confirm no symbol needs a legend
- remove any object that does not strengthen the joke

## User's Preferred Direction

- thoughtful analyst at work or inside an absurd system
- clean white background
- relaxed magazine-cartoon elegance
- visible but purposeful vermillion red
- black outlines with no heavy rendering

Do not copy the sample's title, creator credit, signature, desk layout, or self-referential cartoonist theme.

## Prompt Phrase Bank

Use:

- "highbrow magazine editorial cartoon"
- "familiar visual fable"
- "pen and ink illustration"
- "light ink wash shading"
- "loose hand-drawn black lines"
- "pure white background"
- "large negative space"
- "selective vermillion red #E34234"
- "restrained black humor"
- "instantly readable without a caption"
- "witty and ironic"

Avoid:

- "photorealistic"
- "corporate vector"
- "cute mascot"
- "detailed dashboard UI"
- "busy infographic"
- "technical engraving"
- "dense mechanical cross-hatching"
- "abstract red target"
